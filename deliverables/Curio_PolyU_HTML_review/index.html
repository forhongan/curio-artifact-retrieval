A transfer hypothesis, tested

A CLIP prompt heuristic does not guarantee a gain with SigLIP2. Our experiment measures the effect in this artifact gallery; it does not identify a causal training-data explanation.

Takeaway: the phrase effect stayed inside ±0.006 — effectively zero for this gallery.

Why sets

A ranked list always produces a fifth result — even when it is irrelevant. We score all 4,692 images and make an explicit membership decision.

The accepted set can contain 0, 12, or hundreds of images — chosen on the development set, reported with precision and recall.

Module D · Image–Image Matching

When the text tower runs out of words.

For rare forms and compound techniques the text tower has no stable visual direction. So the system sends the formal name, period and technique to museum APIs, takes back real collection photographs as visual prototypes, and ranks the local gallery by image features.
